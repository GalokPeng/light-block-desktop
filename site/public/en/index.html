01 / WALLPAPER

Static, GIF and video wallpapers

Import an image, GIF or video and keep a full-quality copy in your wallpaper library. Pick a display and fit mode, and find videos by their frame-based thumbnails.

  • Video wallpapers start muted. Toggle sound in Playback Settings or the desktop context menu.
  • Restore the last wallpaper at sign-in. Pause live wallpaper on full screen, lock, display off or battery power.
  • Browse 20 images, 10 GIFs and 20 videos online. Search, preview and download original files with author and license details.

02 / CAPTURE & RECORDING

Annotate screenshots and edit recordings

Press Ctrl + Alt + A to pick a window or region. Annotate a screenshot, stitch a scrolling region, copy a PNG or pin the capture in place.

  • Finished recordings open in a pinned preview. Drag either trim handle and use arrow keys for frame-by-frame precision.
  • Choose quality and frame rate, then export MP4, GIF or lossless MKV. GIF offers fast and fine palette modes.

03 / ORGANIZER & TASKBAR

Organize the desktop and style the native taskbar

Place the organizer at the top, center or bottom. Sort by name, modification time or group, and search for desktop items. Turning the organizer off restores the original icon visibility.

  • Optional taskbar styling centers power and search, apps and the system tray on the primary display on Windows 11 22H2 or later, x64. Windows Search and tray menus stay available.
  • The bar can hide when a full-screen app or window covers it. Move the pointer to the bottom edge to reveal it.
  • Open Taskbar Settings or Task Manager beside the power button. New desktop files appear as icons first; right-click to put them away or choose a group.

04 / DESKTOP NOTES

Keep your to-dos on the desktop

Right-click the desktop background and choose Light Block Desktop · New Note to pin a note. On Windows 11, open Show more options first. Drag the note to move it, or double-click to edit.

  • Search, set reminders, mark tasks done or archive them in Notes & Tasks.
  • Notes save locally. Pinned notes stay visible when you close Settings.

GET STARTED

Install on Windows

Download and run the latest Setup. Setup and the app both use your Windows display language. The portable ZIP is available on the release page.

Download Windows Setup ↓
How do I upgrade?

Click Update now in the app sidebar to download with progress. After verifying Setup, the app exits and starts the installer, preserving existing settings. You can also download Setup manually.

How do I start recording?

Starting with v0.7.4, the installer and portable package include the recording component. Press Ctrl+Alt+A, select a region, then click Record on the toolbar. After stopping, preview and export as MP4, GIF, or lossless MKV.

Where is my data stored?

Settings and wallpapers live in the data folder beside the app. The first launch copies your previous settings. A read-only app folder uses %LOCALAPPDATA%\StillWallpaper.

How is the language selected?

The app follows the Windows display language by default. Switch between English and Simplified Chinese in the upper-right corner of Settings. Setup also includes Traditional Chinese.
